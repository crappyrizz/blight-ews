# Farmer usability session (NFR-04, manual check M-33)

A short, structured session with the collaborating farmer on the app's usability and on whether he understood
what it told him. It is not a field validation of the risk or the diagnosis: the sensor node was not deployed
at the plot. About 20 minutes, on the farmer's own phone with the installed app, logged in to his account.

## Before starting

- Explain the purpose in one sentence: *"I want to see whether the app is easy to use and clear, not to test
  you. You can stop at any time."*
- Ask for consent to note his answers, and that they may appear in the project report **without his name or
  phone number**. Record the date and that consent was given (below). The written consent already given under
  Kenya's Data Protection Act (2019) covers his data in the system; this adds consent for the session itself.
- Do not explain the screens beforehand. If he asks for help during a task, give the smallest hint, and note it.

## Tasks (he does them; you watch and note)

| # | Task, as said to him | Done without help? | Hints given | Time (approx.) | What he said or did |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | "Find out what the app says about the blight risk on your plot today." | | | | |
| T2 | "Check a potato leaf with the app." (any leaf; up to three photos) | | | | |
| T3 | "Find what happened on your plot over the last days." (History) | | | | |

## Questions (after the tasks; write his own words)

1. What did the app tell you about today's blight risk? Why did it say that?
2. After checking the leaf, what did the app tell you? How sure was it?
3. If the app told you the risk was high, what would you do? (Listen for: check the crop, decide himself;
   the app is decision support, never an instruction to spray.)
4. Was anything confusing, hard to read or hard to find?
5. Would you use this during the season? What would make it more useful?

Then, on a scale of 1 (disagree) to 5 (agree), read each and note his answer:

| Statement | 1-5 |
|---|---|
| The app was easy to use. | |
| I understood what the risk level meant. | |
| I understood why the app gave its diagnosis. | |

Afterwards, fill in M-33 in `docs/manual_app_checklist.csv`: **Actual** = tasks completed without help (for
example "3 of 3, one hint on T3"), the Likert answers and the main comment; **Pass/Fail** = Pass if all three
tasks were completed and answers 1-3 show the risk and diagnosis were understood as decision support,
otherwise Fail with the reason; **Date**. One participant cannot show general usability: report it as a single
user's feedback.
