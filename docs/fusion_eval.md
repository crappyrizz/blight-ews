## What this shows

- **3 of 324 test images** have a late-blight probability between the lowest and highest threshold (0.44 to 0.76), so only their call can change with the weather. Every other image is flagged, or not, whatever r is.
- Those images: late_blight_00363.jpg (true late_blight, p_late 0.626); late_blight_00166.jpg (true late_blight, p_late 0.679); late_blight_00633.jpg (true late_blight, p_late 0.586).
- On PlantVillage the CNN is nearly always very sure (clean leaves on plain backgrounds), so the weather has little left to decide here. Fusion is designed for the uncertain middle: field photos against soil and foliage, which `ml/eval_cnn.py` measures once the farmer's photos exist. Re-run this sweep on those to see the effect where it matters.
- Direction, as designed: higher r lowers tau, so recall can only rise and precision can only fall (or stay the same) as the weather turns favourable.
